Ingredients

  • 1 packet of ramen noodles
  • 2 cups of water
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 cup chopped vegetables
  • 1 small garlic clove, chopped
  • Chopped vegetables [eg: green onions, carrots, etc]
  • Chicken strips, boiled
  • 1 teaspoon soy sauce
  • Salt and pepper or other seasonings for taste
  • Spring onions for toppings

Preparation

  1. Boil 2 cups of water in a container
  2. Add the garlic and chopped veggies
  3. Add the ramen noodles and cook according to the packet instructions
  4. Add the soy sauce, salt, pepper and other seasonings
  5. Boil the egg and add in the main dish

ETA Cooking: 15 minutes
